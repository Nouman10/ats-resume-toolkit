    Why resumes get filtered

    Keywords are matched, not understood

    Most systems look for literal terms from the posting. “Project management” and “managed projects” are not the same string.

    Layout can break parsing

    Columns, tables, text boxes, and graphics are common causes of scrambled or empty extractions.

    Missing sections read as missing experience

    If a section can't be identified, its contents can be dropped — even when the experience is right there.
